Lead Generation Flow

Built & orchestrated end-to-end with Claude Code — the agent brain that wrote the scripts, ran every scraper & API, did the web research, and scored the leads.
THE PROBLEM

A list that just fits the ICP isn't enough. There's no signal for which companies actually need a cold-email tool right now — and a big chunk of scraped emails bounce, so reps can't even reach them.

WHAT IT WAS COSTING

Reps burn time on cold accounts with no timing signal, and the best-fit prospects get worked by a competitor first.
